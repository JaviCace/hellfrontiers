# Description

## Short Description 

A fallen angel in hell fights to survive in a pixelated world.

## Long Description 

You were an angel. Then you fell.

Hell Frontiers is a single-player pixel-art action-platformer for Android. After an accident sends you plunging into Hell, your power shatters into fragments. Each fragment becomes a powerful being that embodies an emotion, and you have lost that feeling along with the power. The only way back to Heaven is to hunt down these bosses and take your emotions back.

FIGHT WITH PRECISION Run, jump and strike in four directions, both on the ground and in mid-air. Your basic attack is a quick semi-circular slash. Fire a fast feather projectile to hit enemies from a distance, and raise an ice shield that absorbs the next hit before it recharges.

BUILD YOUR LOADOUT WITH SKILL ORBS Collect skill orbs on your journey and choose which two to equip. Switch between them in the middle of a fight to adapt to what the boss throws at you. Orbs let you:

Hit harder
Unlock the ranged feather attack
Summon the ice shield
Dash
Run faster
Jump higher
Extend your melee range
Recover health when you defeat an enemy

You can only swap which orbs are equipped at a checkpoint, so plan ahead before each fight.

FACE BOSSES BORN OF EMOTION Every boss rules its own zone of Hell and represents one emotion, such as wrath or sadness. Each zone has its own color theme, so red burns with anger and blue weighs with sorrow, always set against the dark of the underworld. Defeat them in order to recover your power.

SOLVE PUZZLES, CLEAR CHECKPOINTS Step on buttons to open doors and work your way through the puzzles that block your path. Activate a checkpoint to restore your health to full. If you fall, you respawn at the last checkpoint you activated.

A LINEAR JOURNEY FROM HELL TO HEAVEN Learn the ropes in the tutorial, defeat the bosses one by one, then return to where you started to open the path to the heavens. A final "floor is lava" climb and one last boss battle stand between you and home.

FEATURES

Handcrafted pixel-art style with a dark, atmospheric palette
Fast melee combat, a ranged feather attack and an ice shield
Collectible skill orbs with two equip slots and in-battle switching
Emotion-themed bosses, each with their own zone and color
Button-and-door puzzles and checkpoints
A final platforming challenge and boss showdown
Single-player experience designed for Android
